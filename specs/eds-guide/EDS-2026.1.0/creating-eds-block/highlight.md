# User Story: Coca-Cola Foundation Environmental Campaign Page

## Epic
As a visitor, I want to learn how The Coca-Cola Foundation is protecting the planet so that I can understand their environmental impact and take action.

---

## Desktop User Story

**As a** desktop user  
**I want to** view a visually engaging campaign section with a background gradient, image, and supporting text side by side  
**So that** I can quickly understand the message and decide whether to learn more.

### Acceptance Criteria

- The section includes a soft background gradient (e.g., light green to soft blue or light neutral to pale green) that reflects an environmental theme.
- The gradient spans the full width of the section.
- The page displays:
  - A large featured image on the left (environmental/landscape visual).
  - A headline on the right: *"Protecting the planet with The Coca-Cola Foundation"*.
  - A short descriptive paragraph explaining the initiative.
  - A clear Call-to-Action (CTA) button (e.g., “See Coca-Cola Foundation”).
- The layout is horizontally aligned (image left, text right).
- The gradient does not reduce text readability (meets accessibility contrast standards).
- The CTA button is clearly visible and clickable.
- The design includes sufficient padding and spacing for a clean layout.

---

## Mobile User Story

**As a** mobile user  
**I want to** view the campaign content in a vertically stacked layout with a background gradient  
**So that** I can easily read the information and interact with the CTA on a smaller screen.

### Acceptance Criteria

- The same background gradient is applied and responsive on mobile.
- The page displays:
  - The featured image at the top.
  - The headline below the image.
  - The descriptive paragraph under the headline.
  - The CTA button below the text.
- All elements are stacked vertically.
- Text remains readable against the gradient background (accessible contrast).
- The CTA button is full-width or easy to tap (minimum 44px heigh
