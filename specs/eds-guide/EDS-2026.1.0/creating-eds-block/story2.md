# User Stories — “You may also like” Recommendations (Desktop + Mobile)

## Story 1 — View recommended content (Desktop)
**As a** website visitor  
**I want** to see a “You may also like” section with recommended cards  
**So that** I can quickly discover related pages to visit next.

### Acceptance Criteria
- A section title “You may also like” is displayed above the recommendations.
- Two recommendation cards are visible side-by-side on desktop.
- Each card includes:
- Background image
- Card title (e.g., “Become part of the Coca‑Cola System”, “Our Investors”)
- Optional short supporting text (if provided)
- A clear click/tap target (card and/or arrow button)
- Clicking a card (or its arrow) navigates to the related destination page.

---

## Story 2 — Navigate recommendations (Desktop)
**As a** website visitor  
**I want** to move through the recommended cards  
**So that** I can view more suggestions beyond the initial set.

### Acceptance Criteria
- Left/right navigation controls are available (e.g., arrow buttons).
- Selecting the right arrow moves to the next set of cards.
- Selecting the left arrow returns to the previous set of cards.
- Navigation does not break the page layout and remains usable at common desktop widths.

---

## Story 3 — View recommended content (Mobile)
**As a** mobile visitor  
**I want** the “You may also like” cards to be easy to read and interact with  
**So that** I can discover related content on a small screen.

### Acceptance Criteria
- The section title is visible above the cards on mobile.
- Cards display in a mobile-friendly layout (e.g., one card per view or stacked).
- Text remains legible and does not overlap important UI elements.
- Tapping a card (or its arrow) navigates to the related destination page.

---

## Story 4 — Swipe through recommendations (Mobile)
**As a** mobile visitor  
**I want** to swipe through recommended cards  
**So that** I can browse multiple suggestions quickly.

### Acceptance Criteria
- Horizontal swipe gestures move between cards.
- Optional arrow controls (if shown) remain tappable and functional.
- The current card is clearly indicated by position (and/or dots if used).
- Swipe navigation is smooth and does not trigger unwanted page scrolling.
